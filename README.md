# Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System

## Project Overview

The Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System is a Salesforce-based application designed to manage multiple types of insurance policies, customers, insurance claims, payments, agents and policy-related activities.

The system provides a centralized platform for managing insurance operations and automating important business processes.

## Objectives

* Manage customer information.
* Manage multiple insurance policies.
* Manage different types of insurance products.
* Register and track insurance claims.
* Track claim status and processing.
* Manage insurance agents.
* Track premium payments.
* Automate policy renewal reminders.
* Automate claim processing activities.
* Provide a centralized insurance dashboard.

## Main Modules

1. Customer Management
2. Insurance Policy Management
3. Policy Type Management
4. Claims Management
5. Insurance Agent Management
6. Payment Management
7. Claim Document Management
