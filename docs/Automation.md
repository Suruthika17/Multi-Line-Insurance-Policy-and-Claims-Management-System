# Automation

## Policy Automation

Policy Trigger validates policy dates.

If the current date is within the policy period, the policy status is automatically set to Active.

If the policy end date has passed, the status is set to Expired.

## Claim Automation

Claim Trigger validates:

- Claim amount
- Approved amount
- Approval status
- Rejection reason

## Flow Automation

Future Salesforce Flows can automate:

- Policy renewal reminders
- Claim processing
- Payment reminders
- Customer notifications
