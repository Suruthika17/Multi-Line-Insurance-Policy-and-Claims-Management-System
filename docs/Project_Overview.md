# Project Overview

## Project Title

Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System

## Introduction

The Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System is a Salesforce-based application designed to manage insurance customers, policies, claims, agents, payments and supporting documents.

The system provides a centralized platform for insurance operations and automates important business processes.

## Objectives

- Manage customer information
- Manage insurance policies
- Manage multiple insurance lines
- Manage insurance claims
- Track claim processing
- Manage insurance agents
- Track premium payments
- Automate policy status management
- Provide dashboard-based monitoring

## Insurance Lines

- Life Insurance
- Health Insurance
- Motor Insurance
- Property Insurance
- Travel Insurance
