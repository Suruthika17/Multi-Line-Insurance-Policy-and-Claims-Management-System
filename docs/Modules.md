# System Modules

## 1. Customer Management

Maintains customer details including contact information and status.

## 2. Policy Management

Creates and manages insurance policies.

## 3. Policy Type Management

Supports multiple insurance categories.

## 4. Claims Management

Allows users to submit, review, approve and reject claims.

## 5. Agent Management

Maintains insurance agent information and policy assignments.

## 6. Payment Management

Tracks policy premium payments and payment status.

## 7. Claim Document Management

Maintains supporting documents related to claims.

## 8. Dashboard

Provides summary information about customers, policies, claims and payments.
